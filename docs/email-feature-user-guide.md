# Your New Email Tab in the MOYD CRM

The CRM now has a built-in email client so you can read and send mail from your `@moyoungdemocrats.org` address without leaving the app.

## What is it?

A full inbox + composer right inside the CRM. Read, reply, compose, attach files, search — all from your dedicated MOYD address. Mail arrives in near-real-time.

## Where do I find it?

Look for the **Email** button in the top nav bar. On mobile, open the hamburger menu (three lines, top-left) — Email is in the drawer.

## What's my email address?

**`firstname@moyoungdemocrats.org`** — the same first name we use for you in the CRM. Anyone can email you there. When you reply or compose, the recipient sees mail from your address (not from a shared inbox).

## Why do I have access when other execs don't yet?

Each of the 15 execs gets a personal address routed through one shared Google Workspace seat behind the scenes — dramatically cheaper than buying everyone a full Google seat, and the CRM enforces that you only ever see your own messages. We provision execs one at a time, so the Email tab appears for each person on rollout.

## How do I read mail?

Open the Email tab. Your inbox loads automatically. Click any message to open the thread. Replies, forwards, and inline images render the same way they would in Gmail.

## How do I send mail?

Click **Compose** (top-left of the inbox). Start typing a recipient — autocomplete suggests people from the CRM and your recent contacts. Add a subject, write your message, drag in attachments if needed, hit **Send**. Sent mail lands in your Sent folder.

## What if I don't see the Email tab?

Your address hasn't been provisioned yet. Ping Andrew and he'll flip the switch.

## Privacy + security

All 15 execs share one Workspace seat under the hood, but the CRM enforces strict separation:

- The server only ever returns messages addressed to your specific alias.
- Sends are signed with your address — you cannot send as another exec.
- Every mail action is logged in the CRM audit trail.
- Other execs literally cannot see your inbox.

## Known limitations (still landing)

- **Vacation responder / auto-reply** — not yet available.
- **View original / download .eml** — landing this week.
- **Mark as answered / forwarded** — Gmail doesn't expose these cleanly; buttons are no-ops.
- **Permanent delete** — moving to trash works; permanent purge does not yet.
- **Mobile push notifications** — desktop updates in near-real-time; native mobile push is on the roadmap.

If anything misbehaves, screenshot it and send Andrew a note. The mail client is brand new and we're polishing actively.

Happy emailing!
